# Deviations from the spec (authoring/SPEC.md) and why

Every point below was forced by what the open data actually contain, was decided before any model saw the panel, and is reported in the README. Nothing was changed after a trial.

1. **Radiation: not laboratory-only.** The spec asked for Cu Kα1 laboratory data only. After harvesting every open pdCIF profile reachable without an account (COD hkl module: 348 pdCIF files, 51 organic with a 2θ profile; Europe PMC supplements of 977 IUCr powder papers 2007–2026: 2 486 structure blocks, 80 usable organic profile+structure pairs), the pool of laboratory organic structures with ≥ 6 DoF, no disorder, no metal and a deposited profile was 24 papers / 28 unique structures, 17 of them at ≥ 10 DoF, and four of those failed the profile–structure pairing check or were flagged. A 30-instance panel therefore contains 17 laboratory instances (15 Cu, 2 Mo) and 13 synchrotron instances. The synchrotron instances are labelled in the truth (`radiation_class`) and results are reported by radiation class; the difficulty evidence in the spec concerns laboratory data, so synchrotron instances are the *easier* end for indexing and should be read that way.

2. **DoF bins.** The spec's ladder (six at 10–15 DoF, fourteen at 15–25, ten at 25–40 / Z′ ≥ 2 / multi-fragment) could not be filled from the open pool. Shipped bins (`dof_bin` in the truth, DoF counted as 6 per rigid fragment in the asymmetric unit, 3 for a monatomic ion, plus RDKit rotatable bonds): `calib_lt15` 10 instances (7–14.5 DoF; four of them below 10), `mid_15_25` 11 instances, `hard_dof25_or_Z2` 9 instances (DoF 26–34 or Z′ = 2). The 2 014 IUCr .rtv deposits behind Cloudflare could not be fetched by script, which is the single largest reason the pool is thin.

3. **Primary source.** The journal site (journals.iucr.org) blocks scripted downloads (Cloudflare challenge), so the profiles and CIFs were taken from Europe PMC's mirror of the same IUCr supplementary material and from the COD hkl module (CC0). The bytes are the depositors' files; the provenance of every instance (PMC id or COD id, file name, block name) is in the private key `pxrd-work/keys/manifest_SECRET.json`.

4. **Reference validation.** The spec wanted only entries that pass the van de Streek & Neumann (2014) DFT-D validation. That study covers IUCr powder structures up to 2013; the shipped panel includes 2014–2026 structures the study never saw. Applied instead, to every instance: (a) any structure matched by cell to a flagged refcode of the 2014 study was excluded (YIRVOL, the crown ether, was in the pool and was removed; UKIRAI's corrected redetermination was not used); the eleven pool structures the study *validated* are noted in the key; (b) a profile–structure pairing check (correlation of the simulated pattern with the background-stripped observed pattern at the stated wavelength versus alternatives; `authoring/evidence/qa_pairing.json`) and a Rietveld-scale check (the deposited structure refined against the shipped pattern with only profile, background, zero and scale free, compared with a random-atoms null; `authoring/evidence/reference_rwp.json`); (c) spglib symmetry search on the reference at 0.1 and 0.3 Å tolerance found no missed higher symmetry for any instance, so Unit L is graded for all 30; (d) no disordered or partially occupied reference is in the panel. Where a paper reported both a powder and a single-crystal structure of the same phase, the powder structure is the graded reference (the cell of Unit L is the cell of the shipped pattern; a single-crystal cell at another temperature would not be).

5. **Composition disclosure.** `composition.json` gives SMILES, counts and charges and a neutral sample description, but not the compound name. The spec's "sample description as reported" would in many cases be the drug name, which is the most direct route to recalling a published cell. The memorisation probe (`authoring/evidence/recall_probe.py`) was run on the SMILES-only composition for both models; one instance (psilocybin Form A) was recalled by GPT-6 Astra to within the Unit L tolerance and was replaced before freezing (by levosimendan, itself probed: not recalled). Fable 5.1 recalled none; four of its "known" claims had wrong cells.

6. **Stereochemistry.** SMILES carry stereodescriptors only when the constitution has a true stereocentre (CIP-based, from the deposited geometry); tags on N, P and S were removed (protonation/tautomer artefacts). For chiral components in Sohncke groups the deposited hand is the graded hand, as the spec requires; for achiral components in Sohncke groups both hands pass.

7. **Identity check fallback.** The spec makes Unit S invalid unless the perceived connectivity matches the SMILES. Distance-based bond perception is fragile to bond-length noise (0.2 Å random jitter broke it on 4 of 30 references at every single slack tried). The grader therefore tries five slack values and, if perception still fails but the symmetry-aware periodic match places every non-hydrogen atom within 1.0 Å (RMSD ≤ 0.35 Å) of a reference atom of the same element, it treats identity as established by the match (a structure that close has the reference's connectivity). Wrong connectivity still fails: it never matches. All defective fixtures (random coordinates, wrong molecule, ring flip, swapped molecules, other polymorph, jitter 0.6 Å) remain at 0 S passes.

8. **Sub-cell loophole closed.** A cell with one axis halved contains lattice vectors matching the reference's, so a mapping search alone would pass it; Unit L additionally requires equal primitive-cell volumes (within 3 %).

9. **Oracle-assisted profiling.** Done with ObjCryst++/FOX through pyobjcryst (parallel tempering, RDKit-built Z-matrix, heavy atoms only, d ≥ 1.7 Å) rather than DASH/GALLOP (DASH has no Linux build; GALLOP on CPU was installed in the image but not used for profiling). Budgets and outcomes are in the README; instances not solved in the profiling budget are **kept and flagged** rather than removed, because removing them would filter the panel on what a particular solver can do in a fixed budget.

10. **Hedging rule detail.** "More than one candidate CIF" is implemented as: any additional file `<id>*.cif` in the submission directory, or a CIF with more than one `data_` block, zeroes the instance.

11. **Points and N.** Score = points / (3 N) with N = 30 as specified; if an instance ever had to be voided, N would drop and the README would say so.
